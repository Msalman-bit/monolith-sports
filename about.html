Sialkot has been making sporting goods since the 1880s, when a British officer asked a local craftsman to repair a tennis racket. We are one of the families that stayed in the trade.

Who we are

Seventy percent of the world's hand-stitched footballs come from our city. We have been making them since 1994.

Monolith Sports began in a rented shed on Wazirabad Road, hand-stitching training balls for a single distributor in Nordrhein-Westfalen. That buyer is still a customer. The shed is now two production units, a testing laboratory and forty-one audited village stitching centres.

We cut, laminate, stitch, mould and test in-house. The business is still family owned, still export-only, and still refuses to subcontract to a floor we cannot walk into unannounced.

Roughly ninety per cent of output goes to the European Union. Every line is built to that specification: CE marking where the PPE Regulation applies, REACH declarations on every consignment, and GPSR technical files ready before the goods leave the floor.

Timeline

Thirty-one years, abbreviated

1994

Founded in Sialkot

Thirty hand-stitchers producing training footballs for a single German distributor.

1998

Atlanta Agreement signatory

Joined the industry-wide programme to eliminate child labour from football stitching, moving all production into monitored centres under IMAC.

2003

ISO 9001 certified

First formal quality management system, audited by TÜV SÜD. Still the backbone of how we run the floor.

2009

Unit II and the testing lab

Thermal bonding presses and an in-house laboratory brought match-ball production and its verification under one roof.

2014

GSP+ and the European pivot

Pakistan's GSP+ status took effect. We closed our non-EU sales desk and rebuilt the business around a single compliance regime.

2016

FIFA Quality Pro licence

Match ball programme licensed after independent testing for weight, sphericity, rebound, water uptake and pressure retention.

2021

Recycled materials line

Global Recycled Standard certification for polyester linings and packaging, driven by buyer commitments rather than regulation.

2025

GPSR and EUDR readiness

Technical files, EU responsible person arrangements and deforestation due-diligence dossiers completed across every SKU.

How we operate

Four commitments
we will put in a contract.

These are not values on a wall. Each one is written into our terms of supply and verifiable by third-party audit.

01

Nothing leaves an unaudited floor

Every stitching centre, moulding line and finishing bench in our supply chain is disclosed on our SEDEX profile and open to unannounced audit by you or your nominated body.

02

The specification is the contract

Approved samples are retained, sealed and referenced by batch. If a shipment deviates from the signed golden sample, we replace it at our cost including freight.

03

Compliance arrives before the goods

Declarations of conformity, test reports, REACH statements and EUDR dossiers are issued at booking, not chased after the container lands.

04

One price, quoted honestly

No mid-programme surcharges for material movement inside a five per cent band. Beyond that we show you the invoice and we split it.
