Health cover for the household

On-site clinic with a full-time nurse, plus registered hospital cover extending to spouse and children.

02

Transport and a hot meal

Company buses on eleven routes across the district, and a subsidised canteen on both sites.

03

A crèche, and it is used

Free childcare at Unit I. It is the single biggest reason our female retention is well above the district norm.

04

Paid skills training

Adult apprenticeships in stitching, moulding and quality inspection, on full pay from day one.

Provident fund and gratuity

Contributory provident fund from month three, and statutory gratuity paid without argument.
