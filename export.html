01 — Tariff

GSP+ means most
of what we make
enters at zero duty.

Pakistan is a beneficiary of the EU's Generalised Scheme of Preferences Plus. For chapter 95 sporting goods, that removes the tariff that would otherwise apply — typically 2.7 % to 4.7 % — on the large majority of lines.

01

We are a registered exporter

Our REX number lets us issue a statement on origin directly on the commercial invoice. No separate Form A, no chamber legalisation delay.

02

Origin is genuinely satisfied

Cutting, lamination, stitching, bonding and finishing all happen in Pakistan, so the goods clear the sufficient-working test comfortably rather than marginally.

03

Your broker claims it on entry

The preference is claimed by the importer at the point of declaration. We supply the statement; your broker enters the preference code. We will brief them if it is their first Pakistani consignment.

GSP+ is conditional on Pakistan's continued ratification and implementation of twenty-seven international conventions, and is reviewed periodically by the European Commission. We quote duty-inclusive landed cost on request so that a change in status is not a surprise on your P&L.

02 — Product rules

Four regulations
that decide whether
you can sell it.

Tariff is the easy part. These are the instruments that actually stop consignments, delist marketplace listings and trigger recalls.

01 GPSR — Regulation (EU) 2023/988 Applicable since 13 December 2024. Requires a technical file, risk assessment, traceability marking and an economic operator established in the EU for every product. We maintain the file and our Hamburg office acts as the named responsible person where you want us to.
02 PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425 Applies to boxing gloves, MMA gloves and shin guards sold as protective equipment. Category II conformity runs through EU type-examination by a notified body. We hold certificates via Notified Body 0598 and CE mark accordingly.
03 REACH — Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 Restricted substances in Annex XVII and candidate-list SVHCs above 0.1 % w/w. Azo dyes, phthalates, PAHs and chromium VI in leather are the live risks in this product category. We lot-test and declare.
04 EUDR — Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 Catches cattle leather and wood. Boxing gloves, cricket bats and cane handles all fall in scope. We supply plot geolocation and a due-diligence statement reference so you can lodge your own declaration in the EU Information System.

06 — Commercial

Payment & currency

We invoice in EUR, USD or GBP. For a first order we will normally ask for a letter of credit or a deposit; after two clean cycles we open terms.

First order

L/C at sight, or 30 / 70

An irrevocable letter of credit at sight through a correspondent bank, or thirty per cent telegraphic transfer with the order and seventy per cent against a scanned bill of lading.

Established

Open account, 30–60 days

After two completed programmes we will consider open account at thirty or sixty days from bill of lading date, subject to a credit limit we agree in writing.

Currency

Fixed for the programme

Prices are held for the agreed production window. We do not apply currency surcharges mid-programme; if the rupee moves violently we absorb it and reprice at renewal.

Tooling

Credited back

Custom moulds and dies are invoiced separately and credited against your first production order once the agreed volume is reached. The tooling remains yours.

Yes. We handle FNSKU labelling, carton marking, pallet configuration and appointment booking for the major EU fulfilment networks. Marketplace listings increasingly require a GPSR responsible person to be shown on the listing itself, and our Hamburg office can be named for that.

There is no minimum shipment as such, only minimum order quantities per product line, which range from 40 kits to 500 balls. Below roughly 12 cubic metres you are better served by LCL on our weekly Rotterdam and Hamburg consolidation than by a part-filled container.

We keep the complete conformity file for every consignment and can send it to your broker within the hour. Most holds on this product category are documentation queries about origin or restricted substances, and both are answerable from the pack. If physical testing is ordered and the goods pass, we split the cost; if they fail on something that is our responsibility, we pay it in full.

For programmes above roughly €400,000 annually we will hold buffer stock in a bonded third-party warehouse near Rotterdam, drawn down against your call-off. It shortens your replenishment from twenty-five days to three and moves the working capital question to a place we can discuss sensibly.

On FOB and EXW terms it is your exposure and your carrier contract. On CIF, CFR, DAP and DDP we quote against a named rate validity, usually thirty days. Beyond that we reprice the freight component only, showing you the carrier invoice. We do not use freight as a hidden margin line.
