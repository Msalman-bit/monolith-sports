Buyer FAQ

The questions we get most often from first-time European buyers, answered without the sales gloss.

Ordering

They vary by line, from 40 agility kits up to 500 hand-stitched balls. Every product page lists its own MOQ. Minimums exist because of set-up time and material lot sizes, not because we are trying to push volume. For a first trial order we will sometimes go below the stated minimum at a higher unit price — ask.

Yes, and most buyers do. Each line still has to meet its own MOQ, but they can share a container and a set of shipping documents. Mixed-SKU master cartons are also possible if you need them for retail distribution.

For a catalogue product with your logo: about two weeks for sampling and approval, then 25 to 35 days production, then 22 to 30 days on the water. Call it ten to twelve weeks from first email to goods on your shelf. Full OEM with new tooling adds six to eight weeks at the front.

Catalogue samples in a standard colourway are free; you pay the courier. Custom samples requiring new patterns or tooling are charged at cost and credited back against your first production order. We do not use sample charges as a revenue line.

Money

An irrevocable letter of credit at sight, or 30 % telegraphic transfer with the order and 70 % against a scanned bill of lading. After two completed programmes we will discuss open account at 30 or 60 days. We are not going to offer open account on a first order, and you should be wary of a supplier who does.

EUR, USD or GBP. Prices are fixed for the agreed production window and we do not add currency surcharges mid-programme. If the rupee moves violently we absorb it and reprice at renewal, with the reasoning shown.

You do, on every Incoterm except DDP. In practice most of our lines attract zero duty under GSP+, so the real cost is your broker's fee and import VAT, which you reclaim. See the HS code table for the specifics.

Quality & compliance

Where the PPE Regulation applies — boxing gloves, MMA gloves and shin guards sold as protective equipment — yes, with EU type-examination through a notified body. Balls, bats, sticks and fitness equipment are not PPE and do not carry CE marking, because affixing it where it does not apply is itself an infringement. Those lines are covered by GPSR instead.

Yes. SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas, QIMA or your own staff. We hold the container until you release it. We charge nothing for hosting an inspection.

Notify us within fifteen working days of arrival with the lot number and photographs. We respond with a root-cause finding within three working days. Where the fault is ours we replace the affected units and pay freight both ways. Sealed golden samples held by both parties are the reference, which is exactly why we insist on producing them.

We can guarantee that every stitching centre is registered, geo-located and open to unannounced inspection by IMAC under the Atlanta Agreement, that we have had no adverse finding since 1998, and that home-based subcontracting is a contractual termination event. That is a stronger claim than a certificate on a wall, and it is verifiable. Read the full position, including its limits.
