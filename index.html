01 — Who we are

Seventy percent of the world's hand-stitched footballs come from our city. We have been making them since 1994.

Monolith Sports is a vertically integrated manufacturer in Sialkot — the Punjab city that has supplied the global game for more than a century. We cut, laminate, stitch, mould and test in-house across two units and forty-one audited village stitching centres.

Our entire output is built to European specification: CE marking where the PPE Regulation applies, REACH substance declarations on every consignment, and GPSR technical files ready before the goods leave the floor.

03 — Manufacturing

From cleft and coil
to a sealed export carton.

Nothing is subcontracted to an unaudited third party. Every process below happens on premises we own, staffed by people on our payroll.

Material intake & laboratory test

Polyurethane sheet, latex, cane, willow and hide arrive against a purchase specification. Our in-house lab runs abrasion, tensile and REACH SVHC screening before anything reaches the cutting floor.

Cutting, lamination & moulding

CNC dies cut panels to ±0.4 mm. Four-ply laminates are pressed and cured, foam cores are injection moulded, and iron is gravity cast at our partner foundry twelve kilometres away.

Stitching & thermal bonding

Forty-one registered village centres hand-stitch under IMAC monitoring. Machine lines and thermal bonding presses run in Unit II for match-grade and high-volume programmes.

Test, certify, pack

Circumference, sphericity, rebound, water uptake and impact attenuation are logged per batch. Technical files, declarations of conformity and carton marking are completed before the container is stuffed.
