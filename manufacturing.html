The process

From cleft and coil
to a sealed export carton.

Nothing is subcontracted to an unaudited third party. Every process below happens on premises we own, staffed by people on our payroll.

Material intake & laboratory test

Polyurethane sheet, latex, cane, willow and hide arrive against a purchase specification. Our in-house lab runs abrasion, tensile and REACH SVHC screening before anything reaches the cutting floor.

Cutting, lamination & moulding

CNC dies cut panels to ±0.4 mm. Four-ply laminates are pressed and cured, foam cores are injection moulded, and iron is gravity cast at our partner foundry twelve kilometres away.

Stitching & thermal bonding

Forty-one registered village centres hand-stitch under IMAC monitoring. Machine lines and thermal bonding presses run in Unit II for match-grade and high-volume programmes.

Test, certify, pack

Circumference, sphericity, rebound, water uptake and impact attenuation are logged per batch. Technical files, declarations of conformity and carton marking are completed before the container is stuffed.

01 / 05 Production sequence
Stage 01 — Cutting & lamination

Panels to ±0.4 mm

Polyurethane sheet is laminated to three, four or five plies of polycotton, cured under press, then cut on CNC dies. Panel tolerance drives sphericity later, so this is where match-ball quality is actually decided.

Stage 02 — Thermal bonding

118 °C, no stitch holes

Six presses bond panel edges directly to the carcass under heat and pressure. Without needle perforations the ball takes on almost no water, which is the whole point of the FIFA Quality Pro immersion test.

Stage 03 — Cut & sew

Ninety-six machine stations

Gloves, guards and bags run through cut-and-sew lines with bar-tack reinforcement at every load point. Latex palms are laid up in a humidity-controlled room to stop premature curing.

Stage 04 — Casting & moulding

Single-pour iron, machined flat

Kettlebells are gravity cast in one pour so there is no weld seam under the hand, then machined to a two per cent tolerance and powder coated for a 400-hour salt spray rating.

Stage 05 — Test & certify

Every batch, logged

Circumference, weight, rebound, sphericity, water uptake and impact attenuation are measured against the signed golden sample. Results are filed by batch and travel with the shipping documents.
