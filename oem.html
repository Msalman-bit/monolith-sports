Development

From brief to
first container.

A realistic timeline for a full OEM programme. White label runs collapse steps two and three into a single week.

Brief & feasibility

Week 1. We tell you what is buildable, what it will roughly cost, and where your specification is fighting itself.

Pattern & costed BOM

Weeks 2–3. Full bill of materials with line-item costs, so you can see which decision is driving the price.

Prototype

Weeks 4–6. A physical sample by courier. Expect two rounds; three is normal on gloves.

Tooling & pre-production

Weeks 7–10. Moulds cut where needed, pilot run of 50–100 units, laboratory verification against the standard.

Golden sample & conformity

Week 11. Sealed samples exchanged, technical file compiled, CE type-examination lodged if the product needs it.

Production & despatch

Weeks 12–16. Bulk run, pre-shipment inspection, stuffing and sailing.

Your intellectual property

We do not sell your
design to anyone else.

This is the single biggest fear European buyers bring to a Pakistani factory, and it is a fair one. Here is how we handle it in writing.

01 Mutual NDA before the first drawing Signed under a governing law you are comfortable enforcing, not ours.
02 Exclusivity on tooling Moulds and dies you have paid for are used only for your orders, and are shipped to you on request.
03 Category exclusivity, negotiable For committed annual volumes we will agree not to supply a directly competing brand in your territory.
04 No overrun sales Surplus and second-grade units carrying your mark are destroyed under witness, not sold into a grey market.
05 Branded components controlled Printed labels, hang tags and embossing dies are counted in and counted out against production numbers.
