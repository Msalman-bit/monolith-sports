Privacy
notice

This is a template notice supplied with the website. Before you publish, have it reviewed against your actual data processing by someone qualified in your jurisdiction.

Who we are

Monolith Sports Industries (Pvt) Ltd, Plot 42–46, Small Industrial Estate II, Wazirabad Road, Sialkot 51310, Punjab, Pakistan, is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Our EU representative for the purposes of Article 27 GDPR is our Hamburg office, Hafenstraße 18, 20359 Hamburg, Germany.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Name, company, work email, telephone number, country and the content of any message or quotation request you submit.
  • Commercial details. Product interest, quantities, target pricing, delivery destination and Incoterm preference, where you provide them.
  • Technical information. Your browser sets a single local storage value to remember your light or dark theme preference. That value never leaves your device and is not a cookie.

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party pixels. Web fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which will receive your IP address as part of that request; if you would rather avoid this, the fonts can be self-hosted and the site will work identically.

Why we process it, and on what basis

  • To answer your enquiry and quote for work. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract, Article 6(1)(b).
  • To perform a contract once agreed. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b).
  • To meet customs, export and product safety record-keeping obligations. Legal basis: legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).
  • To send the trade bulletin, where you asked for it. Legal basis: consent, Article 6(1)(a), withdrawable at any time.

Who sees it

Your details are seen by our export desk and, where relevant, our compliance and production teams. We share data with freight forwarders, customs brokers and inspection bodies only where necessary to fulfil your order. We do not sell enquiry data, and we do not pass it to other manufacturers.

International transfer

Data submitted through this site is transferred to Pakistan, which is not the subject of a European Commission adequacy decision. Transfers are made under Standard Contractual Clauses together with supplementary technical measures. A copy of the clauses is available on request.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: 24 months from last contact.
  • Customer records and contracts: 10 years, matching the technical documentation retention required under EU product safety law.
  • Bulletin subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
  • Job applications: 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of what came before.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@monolithsports.example. We respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to your national supervisory authority; in Germany that is the Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Security

Enquiry data is transmitted over TLS and stored in access-controlled systems. Access is limited to staff who need it for the purpose described above. We will notify affected individuals and the relevant supervisory authority of a personal data breach where the law requires it.

Changes

We will post any revision here with an updated date at the top. Material changes affecting existing customers will be notified by email.
