01 EU Declaration of Conformity Signed per product family, listing the harmonised standards applied and the notified body where type-examination was required.

03 REACH SVHC statement Declaration against the current candidate list, refreshed each time ECHA updates it rather than annually.

Escalation

If something is wrong

Notify us with the lot number and photographs within fifteen working days of arrival. We respond with a root-cause finding inside three working days. Where the defect is ours, we replace the affected units and pay the freight both ways. There is no argument about who absorbs the cost — it is written into our terms of supply.

Yes, and we would prefer you did. You can attend in person, send an agent, or nominate SGS, Intertek, Bureau Veritas or QIMA. We hold the container until you release it. Third-party inspection is billed at cost with no handling margin added.

Two sealed samples are produced at approval. One stays with us in the quality office, one goes to you. Both carry the same reference and date. Production is measured against ours; disputes are settled against yours. Samples are retained for the life of the programme plus two years.

Across 2025 our customer-reported defect rate was 0.31 % of units shipped, and our internal reject rate before despatch was 2.7 %. The second number being much larger than the first is the point of having a laboratory. We publish both figures in our annual buyer report.

Our SEDEX profile can be linked to your account, which gives you the SMETA report directly. amfori BSCI results are shareable through the platform. ISO certificates and surveillance audit summaries are sent on request. Unannounced visits need no notice — that is what unannounced means.
